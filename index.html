A home for every worshipper

Okeiyanuoluwa means “The Mountain of the Lord’s joy”. It is a place where everyone comes to meet God, find peace and be encouraged regardless of gender, tribe or nationality.

We welcome everyone, whether you are a lifelong member or visiting for the first time.

Come in truth, come as you are, and join us in song, prayer and the teaching of the Word.

“I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord.” Psalm 122:1
